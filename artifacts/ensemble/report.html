Figures

  1. Population trajectories across ensemble models (thin lines) and the diagnostic model (red).
  2. Spawning depletion by model and region, relative to each model's dynamic unfished reference.
  3. Planned and retained model inputs.
  4. Weighted distributions of management quantities by design setting. Boxes: interquartile range; line: median; whiskers: 2.5th and 97.5th weighted percentiles.
  5. Weighted distributions of management quantities by design setting. Boxes: interquartile range; line: median; whiskers: 2.5th and 97.5th weighted percentiles.
  6. Population trajectories with supplied uncertainty intervals. Central 50, 80, 95% intervals (illustrative model spread and parameter perturbations).
  7. Kobe (left) and Majuro (right) status in 2022. Open points: model estimates; percentages: weighted status probabilities. Contours: 50%, 80% and 95% joint highest-density regions of supplied joint uncertainty draws. Majuro probabilities distinguish depletion below the limit, and F above or below FMSY when the depletion criterion is met.
